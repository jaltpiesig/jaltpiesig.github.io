<div class="about-content-section" style="background: #ffffff; padding: 30px; border-radius: 8px; border: 1px solid var(--border-subtle); margin-bottom: 30px;">
  <h2 class="section-title">Our Mission & Vision</h2>
  <p style="margin-bottom: 15px;">
    Originally founded in 2011 as the Speech, Drama, & Debate SIG, our community expanded to embrace a wide spectrum of creative and collaborative classroom performances. We aim to provide language educators with practical tools, research-backed methodologies, and a collaborative network to bring performance into language learning environments across Japan and internationally.
  </p>

  <h2 class="section-title" style="margin-top: 25px;">What We Do</h2>
  <ul style="margin-left: 20px; line-height: 1.8; margin-bottom: 15px;">
    <li><strong>Conferences & Workshops:</strong> Hosting annual PanSIG strands, JALT national presentation sessions, and independent webinars.</li>
    <li><strong>Publications:</strong> Publishing regular newsletter issues and academic articles featuring practical lesson ideas, teacher reflections, and research.</li>
    <li><strong>Podcast Series:</strong> Interviewing leading practitioners and researchers in performance-based teaching.</li>
    <li><strong>Grants & Awards:</strong> Supporting member research and project initiatives that promote creative performance in education.</li>
  </ul>
