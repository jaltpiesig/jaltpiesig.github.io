We’re excited to announce that the Second Episode of PIE SIG's monthly podcast is now available on Apple Podcasts, [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/episode/5f3pq2xQ5pctBQYMCr6az3?si=80b2b8a17e674cd7), and other major platforms.

In this episode, I speak with Ashley Ford-Mihashi, lecturer at Nagoya City University and organizer of the Reel Voices Language Learning Film Festival. Ashley reflects on her early experiences with music and language, and discusses how filmmaking and performance can give quiet learners a stronger voice.

We encourage you to:  
– Listen and follow the podcast on your preferred platform  
– Leave a rating or comment to help others discover it  
– Share the episode with colleagues interested in performance in education

New episodes will be released monthly, featuring educators who use performance to make teaching more engaging and effective.

Thank you for your continued support of the PIE SIG. 

Darren Kinsman  
Host and Producer, The PIE SIG Podcast
