## 📌 Conference Overview

* **Event**: Full STEAM Ahead!
* **Topic**: [STEAM (Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, Mathematics)](https://www.ucf.edu/online/engineering/news/comparing-stem-vs-steam-why-the-arts-make-a-difference/)
* **Plenary Speaker**: Tom Robb (Professor Emeritus, Kyoto Sangyo University; Editor-in-Chief, *TESL-EJ.org*; Chair, *The Extensive Reading Foundation*)
* **Gold Sponsors**: 
  * [The Performance in Education SIG](https://jaltpiesig.org)
  * [The Mind, Brain, and Education SIG](https://www.mindbrained.org/the-brain-sig/)
  * [JALTCALL SIG](https://jaltcall.org)
  * [The Okinawa Chapter of JALT](https://okijalt.org)
* **Supported By**: Oxford University Press

* **Lawson** (Naha Nishi 1-chome \| ~10-min walk)

### Local Transportation Tips
* **Kokusai Street:** ~15-min bus ride from Miegusuku bus stop.
* **Shuri Castle:** ~20-min monorail ride via Asahibashi Station.
* **Shintoshin Area (New Urban Center):** ~15-min monorail ride via Omoromachi Station. Major hub featuring *San-A Naha Main Place*, *Okinawa Prefectural Museum & Art Museum*, and *Shintoshin Park*.
