<h4 style="color: #0066cc; margin-top: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">📚 Publications Chair Updates</h4>
<p style="line-height: 1.7; color: #334155;">
We are transitioning to a rolling submission process for <em>Mask & Gavel</em> and <em>Classroom Resources</em>:
</p>
<ul style="line-height: 1.8; color: #334155; padding-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 15px;">
  <li><strong>Year-Round Submissions:</strong> Submit at any time without waiting for an annual deadline.</li>
  <li><strong>Rapid Online Publication:</strong> Peer-reviewed and finalized papers will immediately be published on our website, adding to your CV faster.</li>
  <li><strong>Biennial Volumes:</strong> Articles published online will be collected into a bound volume every two years.</li>
</ul>
<p style="font-size: 0.95em; color: #475569;">
