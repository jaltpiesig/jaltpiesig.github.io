## Performance in Education: Tools for Your Toolbox

**Fourth Annual PIE SIG in Sapporo Conference: Performance in Education: Tools for Your Toolbox**

**Date**: (Saturday, Feb 15, 2025)

**Site**: The PIE conference will be held Saturday, February 15th at the [SCARTS Sapporo](https://www.sapporo-community-plaza.jp/scarts.php) [](https://www.sapporo-community-plaza.jp/scarts.php)Cultural Arts Community Center

**Theme**: Performance in Education: Tools for Your Toolbox

[Call for Papers](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSc0zf8JCjTOVJH5CbWsxJCRbtVBwibe2fM20JkGTMOYbL6u1w/viewform)  
All PIE SIG-related proposals for this face-to-face conference need to be sent by Monday, November 25th, 2024 23:59 (JST).

[Registration](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdbMBmD-3iSgEBMthAWjwbqVPtBHKzFdqQx7In8YKbQ8kQy3w/viewform)

This face-to-face conference will be held in cooperation with the [40th Annual JALTHokkaido Conference 2025: Teaching Tools for the Classroom](https://jalt.org/event/hokkaido/25-02-15) _(click here for [Call for Papers for Sunday conference at Hokkai Gakuen University](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeT2IsDuCUN2UioqCpOhauH_YIpG4BnGZ-aE8r_Tx2X1q9Hhw/viewform)),_ and we encourage attendees to check out both conferences at different venues with different conference fees for each one. JALT Hokkaido and the Performance in Education (PIE) JALT Special Interest Group are hosting a two-day conference at two locations in Sapporo:

- PIE SIG: Saturday, February 15th, Sapporo Cultural Arts Community Center (SCARTS)  
    

- JALT Hokkaido: Sunday, February 16th: Hokkai-Gakuen University

Saturday will be primarily for PIE presentations. On Sunday, PIE SIG will also have slots available for PIE presentations at the JALT Hokkaido Conference. 

See you in Sapporo!
