The Performance in Education (PIE) SIG had an active presence at <a href="https://pansig.org/2024" target="_blank" style="color: #0066cc; text-decoration: none; font-weight: 600;">JALT's PanSIG 2024</a>, held at <strong>Fukui University of Technology</strong> from May 24–26, 2024.
</p>
