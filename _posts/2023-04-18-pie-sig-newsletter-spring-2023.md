<h4 style="color: #0066cc; margin-top: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">📚 Publications Updates (Philip Head)</h4>
<p style="line-height: 1.7; color: #334155;">
Thanks to a research grant obtained by David Kluge, two PIE publications are being mailed to members in <strong>print form</strong> this week:
</p>
<ul style="line-height: 1.8; color: #334155; padding-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 15px;">
  <li><strong>Performance in Education Review 2020:</strong> Collected papers from past PIE SIG conference presentations.</li>
  <li><strong>Classroom Resources Volume 2:</strong> Practical classroom activities for teachers using PIE methods.</li>
</ul>

<p style="line-height: 1.7; color: #334155;"><strong>New Rolling Submissions Policy:</strong></p>
<ul style="line-height: 1.8; color: #334155; padding-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 15px;">
  <li><strong>Year-Round Submissions:</strong> We are now accepting submissions for <em>Mask & Gavel</em> and <em>Classroom Resources</em> year-round.</li>
  <li><strong>Faster CV Publishing:</strong> Peer-reviewed articles will be posted immediately to our website upon completion.</li>
  <li><strong>Biennial Volumes:</strong> Online papers will be gathered into a bound print volume every two years.</li>
</ul>

<p style="font-size: 0.95em; color: #475569;">
👉 Read guidelines at <a href="https://jaltpiesig.org/publications/" target="_blank" style="color: #0066cc; font-weight: bold;">jaltpiesig.org/publications</a> or send submissions to <a href="mailto:piesigpublications@gmail.com" style="color: #0066cc;">piesigpublications@gmail.com</a>.
</p>

</p>

</ul>
