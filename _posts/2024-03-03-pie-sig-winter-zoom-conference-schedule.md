The PIE SIG Winter Zoom Conference will be held on Saturday, March 16, 2024, from 09:00 to 18:00. The conference theme is _Recipes for PIE: MY PIE Activity_.

If you would like to register to present at the Winter Zoom Conference, [please click here to go to our Call for Papers](https://jaltpiesig.org/call-for-papers-pie-sig-winter-zoom-my-activity-conference-march-16-2024/). If you would like to register to attend the Winter Zoom Conference, please sign up [at this link](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfY8nWd0Hz9U6vgPIH5HUvif-fYv9swdtrEP1nlpOKxETkLhw/viewform) by Thursday, March 14, 2024.
