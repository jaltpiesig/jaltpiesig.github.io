The Performance in Education Special Interest Group (PIE SIG) will host a free, one-day Zoom conference. We welcome anyone interested in demonstrating various learning activities to submit an application through our Call for Papers [by clicking here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfrQD9VS0bDT3dVzTf_cFZTMh8brvQpjB-IN5giNZfIAZNuqg/viewform).

All presentations will be 25 minutes long and should be a Performance in Education activity that can be shared with everyone. Twenty-five-minute Performance in Education performances are also welcome. Submissions should consist of a title and an abstract of 150-200 words.

If you are interested in presenting, please submit your application by Sunday, March 3, 2024 JST. Notifications of abstract status will be sent by Monday, March 4, 2024.
