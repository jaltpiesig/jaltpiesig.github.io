We’re excited to announce that the PIE SIG has launched its new monthly podcast. The first episode is now available on Apple Podcasts, [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/episode/5f3pq2xQ5pctBQYMCr6az3?si=80b2b8a17e674cd7), and other major platforms.

In this debut episode, David Kluge—founder of the PIE SIG—talks about how performance techniques like Readers’ Theatre and music can increase student engagement and retention.

We invite you to:  
– Listen and follow the podcast on your preferred platform  
– Leave a comment or rating to help others find it  
– Share the episode with colleagues or anyone interested in performance in education

A new episode will be released each month, featuring conversations with educators using performance to make teaching more effective.

Thank you for being part of the PIE SIG. 

Warm regards,

Darren Kinsman  
Host and Producer, The PIE SIG Podcast
