We’re excited to announce that Episode 3 of PIE SIG’s monthly podcast is now available on Buzzsprout: [https://www.buzzsprout.com/2520024/episodes/17851693](https://www.buzzsprout.com/2520024/episodes/17851693)

It is also be available on our website, Apple Podcasts, [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/episode/5f3pq2xQ5pctBQYMCr6az3?si=80b2b8a17e674cd7), and other major platforms.

In this episode, I speak with Juilliard-trained educator Kevin Bergman, with 30+ years directing over 100 student dramas, reveals how to transform language learning and build social skills, plus practical strategies teachers can use immediately.

Kevin Bergman's annotated bibliography, mentioned in the podcast, can be found here:

[Bergman Episodes 3/4 Annotated Bibliography](https://jaltpiesig.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Bergman-Episodes-34-Annotated-Bibliography.pdf)
