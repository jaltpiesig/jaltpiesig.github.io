## 3rd Annual PIE-in-Nagoya Conference

**Conference Date:** 15-16 November

**Conference Theme:** PIE for Every Occasion

**PIE Activities:** Speech, presentation, drama, debate, etc. for either normal occasions like a class or school festival or special occasions like Halloween, Thanksgiving, Christmas, Tanabata, etc

**Conference Venue:** [Moriyama Life-long Learning Center](https://www.city.nagoya.jp/kyoiku/page/0000051823.html)

![](images/Picture-5.jpg)

[Call for Submissions](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSe8OVhrdY7YPbB0ZtETDQmyVzD7l6qAqb2AF0L1QlW1df1bDQ/viewform)

**Day 1 (Saturday evening, 15 November):**  9:00 Registration, 10:00-17:00 Presentations, \*6:30-8:30 Networking Dinner: [Ryusenji no Yu Supersento Sauna and Hot Spring Baths](https://www.ryusenjinoyu.com/moriyama/) (\*Banquet time is 2 hours from time of arrival from the conference site).

**Day 2 (Sunday, 16 November)**: 10:00-15:30 Presentations, 15:30-16:30 Clean-up, 17:30 Networking/Officers Evaluation and Planning Meeting
