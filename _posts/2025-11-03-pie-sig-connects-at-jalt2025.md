<h3 style="margin-top: 0; color: #1e293b; border-bottom: 2px solid #f1f5f9; padding-bottom: 10px;">🌟 Highlights from Tokyo</h3>

<p style="font-size: 1.05em; line-height: 1.7; color: #334155;">
At JALT2025 in Tokyo, the <strong>Performance in Education (PIE) SIG</strong> had a solid presence, engaging educators interested in drama, voice, and movement in language teaching.
</p>

<p style="font-size: 1.05em; line-height: 1.7; color: #334155;">
The PIE Forum was MCed by PIE SIG founder and Conferences Chair, <strong>David Kluge</strong>:
</p>

<div style="background-color: #f8fafc; border-left: 4px solid #7e22ce; border-radius: 4px; padding: 16px; margin: 20px 0; font-style: italic; color: #475569;">
  “The room was small and many of the forum presentations were workshops that required movement, so the room was always filled with excitement and laughter. It was magical.”
</div>

<p style="font-size: 1.05em; line-height: 1.7; color: #334155;">
Over three <em>“Meet the SIGs”</em> sessions, our table drew steady interest. Attendees asked questions, copied our podcast QR code, and <strong>4–5 people committed to joining the SIG!</strong> Several attendees also expressed plans to present at or attend our upcoming regional conferences.
</p>

</div>
