<h3 style="margin-top: 0; color: #1a202c;">Description</h3>
<p><strong>PIE SIG at Lake Nojiri</strong></p>
<p>This is the PIE SIG Conference in the beautiful resort area of Lake Nojiri in northern Nagano. The site of the 3-day conference on Performance in Education is right on the lake.</p>
<p>PIE SIG conferences have always been family-friendly events, and this conference is no exception. The Lake Nojiri area is a place that has everything necessary for a great vacation: impressive natural scenery, interesting sights to see, a variety of family entertainment spots, many good restaurants, and many types of accommodations from top-class hotels to pensions and campgrounds. Come to the conference with your family!</p>
<p><em>Weather Note: The weather has been cool in the morning and evening and warm in the afternoon. Lately it has been rainy, so please bring a raincoat and umbrella.</em></p>
